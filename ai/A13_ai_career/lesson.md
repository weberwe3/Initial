# Module A13: Your AI Career

**Goal:** turn this curriculum into career capital: a clear target role, a portfolio that proves your skills, and a story that positions you for AI-related positions.
**Time:** 2 hours to plan, then a 90-day plan to execute.

You're in a strong position. Business analysts who combine **domain knowledge,
data skills (Alteryx, SQL), Python and practical AI** are exactly who
organisations need to turn AI from demos into business results. This module
helps you make that visible.

---

## 1. Roles this curriculum points toward

| Role | What it involves | What to emphasise |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| **AI-enabled business / data analyst** | Analysis with AI throughout; text analytics; automation | Measurable time savings; text analytics projects; verification habits |
| **AI enablement lead / AI champion lead** | Adoption programmes, training, governance, prompt libraries | Ambassador results: adoption %, training delivered, use cases scaled |
| **Analytics engineer / automation developer** | Production data pipelines with AI steps (Alteryx, Python, SQL, cloud) | Reliable pipelines; testing; evaluation; cost control |
| **AI product analyst / AI product owner** | Defining AI features, success metrics, evaluation, rollout | Use-case discovery, evals, stakeholder communication |
| **AI solutions consultant (internal or external)** | Scoping and delivering AI use cases for business teams | Breadth: tools, RAG, agents, governance; storytelling |

Titles vary wildly between companies. Read job descriptions for the **skills**
they ask for, not the title.

## 3. A portfolio that proves it

Three to four solid projects beat ten tutorials. Strong choices for a BA in an
Alteryx shop (use public or synthetic data, or anonymised work examples you're
allowed to share):

1. **Customer-feedback insights pipeline:** AI classification of thousands of
   comments, evaluated against a gold set, with a keyword baseline comparison
   and a one-page insight report. (This track's capstone.)
2. **Alteryx ↔ Python translation:** a real-style workflow rebuilt in pandas,
   with row-count reconciliation and a write-up of when you'd use each.
3. **Policy/document Q&A (RAG):** a small grounded Q&A over public documents
   with citations and a 30-question evaluation.
4. **Ambassador case study:** the use-case workshop, prioritisation, pilot
   design and measured results (anonymised), as a 2-page write-up.

For each project, show: **the business problem, your approach, how you
verified it, the result in numbers, and what you'd do next.** Put code on
GitHub with a clear README (the Python track's capstone teaches this).

## 4. Telling your story

Use **situation → action → result**, with numbers:

> "Our team spent ~6 hours a week manually tagging survey comments, so we only
> sampled 10%. I built an AI classification step into our Alteryx workflow,
> evaluated it at 91% agreement with expert labels, and now we analyse 100% of
> comments in under an hour. As AI ambassador I trained 14 colleagues; weekly
> active usage went from 3 to 11 people."

Keep a running **brag document**: every win, metric, quote and lesson,
dated. It writes your performance review, your CV bullets and your interview
answers.

---

## 5. Credentials (optional, useful for signalling)

Credentials don't replace a portfolio, but they help get past filters:

- **Your platforms:** Alteryx's own certifications validate the skills you
  use daily.
- **Cloud AI fundamentals:** entry-level AI certifications from major cloud
  providers (Microsoft, Google, AWS) are widely recognised.
- **Vendor courses:** AI model providers and learning platforms offer free or
  low-cost courses on prompting, APIs and agents.

Pick one or two that match your target role and your company's technology stack.

---

## 6. Staying current without drowning

- Follow **two or three** trusted sources, not twenty.
- Re-test your prompt library when models change; keep what still wins.
- Spend 30 minutes a week trying one new capability on a real task.
- Share what you learn: teaching is the fastest way to deepen it, and it's
  visible leadership.

| Days | Focus | Output |
|------|-------|--------|
| 1–30 | Daily AI use; A00–A04; guardrail card; prompt library v1 | Evidence log; first team demo |
| 31–60 | A05–A08; capstone pipeline on a real or public dataset | Evaluated pipeline; write-up |

At day 90, update your CV and internal profile, share the pilot results, and
have a conversation with your manager about where AI fits in your next role.

- Tell stories with situation → action → result, and keep a brag document.
- A 90-day plan turns this curriculum into visible career capital.
